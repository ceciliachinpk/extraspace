The journey

Five phases, in the order a personal-storage customer moves through them.

Entry

Find

Convert

  • Step 1 of 2 — name, mobile, email, move-in date
  • Step 2 of 2 — reference number, rate held 7 days
  • No account needed to get this far

Sign in

  • Steps 1–3 shown throughout; password is the fallback, not the front door
  • Returns you to wherever you started

Manage

  • Extend — 6 or 12 months, 5 % off for a year
  • End booking — 10 days’ notice enforced, reason asked, 6 pm acknowledgement
  • After ending, the unit shows “Move-out scheduled” and can be cancelled
